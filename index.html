05 / THE DETAILS

BEFORE
WE TALK.

Marketing should be easy to understand. Here are a few useful starting points.

Do you only work with dentists?

Dental practices are my focus. The results shown here come from my work in other industries, and I am upfront about that.

Do you guarantee new patients?

No ethical marketing partner can guarantee patient volume. The focus is on thoughtful strategy, relevant campaigns, stronger visibility and tracking the right inquiries when data is available.

Do we need to appear on camera?

Founder and team participation can build familiarity and trust, but the content plan should fit your comfort level, schedule and applicable patient privacy requirements.

Can you help beyond social media posts?

Yes. The broader offering includes Meta advertising, reputation management, customer insight, websites and automation planning, with scope defined together.

How do we get started?

Start with a conversation about the practice, its current marketing, patient inquiry sources and priorities. That determines a realistic scope and next steps.
