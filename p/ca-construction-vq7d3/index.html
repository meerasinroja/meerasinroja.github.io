Target customer

Who the customer really is.

The target customer is a homeowner with money who needs a major job done right. They want more space, a better home, a place for parents or adult children to live, or a backyard home they can rent out.

“ They worry about getting ripped off. They fear bad work, failed city inspections, delays, surprise costs, and a builder who disappears after taking payment.

Current issue / gap in selling

“We do quality work. We handle the process. Call us.”

That message is too vague because every builder says they do quality work. Right now, CA Construction Group sounds too much like every other builder and does not clearly show its competitive advantage.

Why the ads likely have not worked.

The ads and content may sound generic instead of focusing on what makes CA Construction different. An ad that says, “Need a backyard home? Get a free quote,” attracts people looking for the cheapest price. Those people compare several builders and choose the lowest bid.
