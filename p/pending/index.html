Next steps

01

Step 1 — Build the Trust Landing Point

A clean, mobile-first website that shows Marca's service area, services (repair, install, maintenance plans), and a clear call-to-action. When a homeowner Googles Marca after a referral, they find a real business, not a blank result.

02

Step 2 — Create the Proof Feed on Instagram

Before-and-after installs, quick repair walk-throughs, and local Culver City content. This is the visual proof that turns a skeptical prospect into a confident caller — especially for $8K–$15K install decisions where homeowners need to trust who they're inviting into their home.

03

Step 3 — Run Tracked Instagram Ads to the Right Zip Codes

Targeted ads reaching homeowners in Culver City and surrounding areas who are in the market for HVAC repair or a new system. Every ad is tracked so Marca knows exactly which spend is producing repair calls and install inquiries — not guesswork.

04

Step 4 — Capture Maintenance Plan Customers Consistently

A simple offer promoted through ads and the website to convert one-time repair customers into $200–$400 recurring annual plan holders — building predictable revenue on top of the job-by-job work.
